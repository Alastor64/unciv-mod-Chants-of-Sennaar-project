# civs from Chants of Sennaar

An Unciv mod adding civilizations from the game *Chants of Sennaar*.

## Civilizations

| Civilization | Leader | Unique name | Unique |
|---|---|---|---|
| Warriors | God | Protectors of Heaven | Supply is doubled; +100% Production when constructing military buildings and wonders in all cities; military units get +20% Strength when defending and heal +10 HP in friendly territory; "Borrows" city names from other civilizations in the game |

The Warriors start with a bias towards Mountains and have unique buildings, units and a tile improvement:

| Type | Name | Replaces | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Building | Fortress | Walls | +2 Production, +2 Faith; military units cost 50% less Production in this city |
| Building | Wine and Spirits Cellar | Shrine | +1 Food and +1 Happiness instead of Faith; +2 Food on Wine, +1 Food on Citrus and +3 Food on Sugar tiles in this city |
| Building | The Arrival of the Chosen | National Epic | National Epic's bonuses plus a free Great Artist and +5 Happiness |
| Building | The Duty of a Warrior | Heroic Epic | Heroic Epic's bonuses plus a free Great General, +3 Faith and +1 Movement for units in friendly territory |
| Unit | Sapper | Worker | Can cross Mountains, takes no damage while on them |
| Unit | Officer | Great General | +30% Strength to military units within 2 tiles when attacking (instead of the usual +15%) |
| Unit | The Chosen | Great Artist | +50% Strength to military units within 2 tiles when defending (instead of starting a Golden Age) |
| Tile improvement | Stargazing Platform | — | Built on Mountains, Science and Culture that grow with technology |

Add the folder to Unciv's `mods` directory (or install it from this repository's URL) and
enable it as an extension mod when starting a new game.
